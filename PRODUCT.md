# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- Developers evaluating the experience their buyers will receive.
- Prospective lot buyers inspecting and comparing lots, primarily on a phone, then contacting the development team.
- Staff and the owner reviewing map content and lot information.

## Product Purpose

Wamule provides an embeddable, interactive lot map for a real-estate development. Buyers should be able to understand lot availability, compare the key facts and price, select a lot, and take a clear next action. The first Demo A is a private proof of concept using visibly labeled sample data; it is not an authoritative sales inventory.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, polished; calm and confident. A significant financial decision should feel safe and easy, not flashy.

The embed chrome is neutral and platform-agnostic. Tenant theme tokens supply the brand colors and type. Do not show a platform wordmark in the embed; a small configurable “powered by” line is acceptable.

Reference qualities (not designs to copy):

- Airline and cinema seat maps: unmistakable available/taken states, tap-to-select, details, and an action.
- Apple Maps and Mapbox-style base maps: a quiet neutral base with a vivid data layer.
- Airbnb/Zillow-style property cards: price, key facts, one clear call to action, and generous spacing.
- Stripe/Linear: restrained, crisp typography and precise spacing.

## Anti-references

- GIS/CAD viewers and raw plats: dense labels, tiny type, and toolbar clutter.
- Rainbow tier colors layered with traffic-light status colors; use one color system per view.
- Countdown timers, false scarcity, fake urgency, or untruthful “in demand” signals.
- A PDF viewer or an image with hotspots instead of a usable map experience.
- Generic admin-dashboard templates, glossy gradients, neon, and luxury-real-estate clichés such as stock gold or stock photography, unless a tenant theme explicitly asks for them.

## Design Principles

- Keep the buyer map phone-first; use desktop as the large-screen showcase, not as the baseline.
- Make lot state, selection, key facts, and the next action immediately understandable without relying on color alone.
- Keep the base map quiet and the selected data layer vivid; do not combine competing tier and status palettes in one view.
- Earn trust with accurate, clearly labeled sample/provenance information and truthful availability; never manufacture urgency.
- Keep the embed platform-neutral and let tenant theme tokens carry the tenant’s brand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Prioritize phone use, keyboard access, readable contrast, non-color status cues, clear focus, usable touch targets, and reduced-motion preferences. Audit the embedded buyer journey, not only the surrounding application shell.
