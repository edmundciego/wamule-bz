Road-width reference review

Record candidate scale evidence from known road widths on the approved source. This page does not approve scale or change parcel geometry.

Use the approved drawing or survey as the authority. Do not infer road widths from typical streets, lot-area medians, or OCR. Measure across the road edges, perpendicular to its centerline, away from intersections and labels.

  1. Measure at least three distinct 60 ft references in each source-image direction. These are the fit references.
  2. Measure at least three distinct 40 ft references in each direction. These are held out to check the fit.
  3. Flag unclear or inconsistent references; do not move clicks to make ratios pass. A passing page is still only a candidate for owner/reviewer approval.
